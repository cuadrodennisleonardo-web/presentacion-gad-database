System Objectives & Value

Transforming Municipal Data Management

Overcoming traditional LGU operational bottlenecks through modern municipal cloud architecture.

Traditional Bottlenecks

  • Fragmented paper forms and separate Excel files in different offices
  • Inconsistent Sex-Disaggregated Data (SDD) required for GAD compliance
  • Time-consuming consolidation during AIP and annual budget planning
  • Risk of lost baseline records during personnel transitions

Centralized Platform

  • Single municipal repository accessible 24/7 with cloud backup
  • Standardized Male and Female tracking by default across all sectors
  • Instant 1-click PDF and CSV reports for Sangguniang Bayan sessions
  • Structured submission and verification flow before data commits

Executive Impact

  • Evidence-based policy formulation and targeted resource allocation
  • Seamless compliance with DILG, DSWD, DepEd, and GAD mandates
  • Clear visibility into sector performance and community needs
  • Standardized records for audits and annual accomplishment reports
Security & Governance

User Access & Permission Structure

Role-based access control ensuring data integrity, institutional security, and clear accountability.

Super Admin

System Administrators / MPDO / IT
  • Full administrative access across all sectors
  • Manage user accounts and department permissions
  • Create & edit tables via Dynamic Tables Manager
  • Review, approve, or reject department data submissions
  • Inspect complete municipal audit logs and history

Department Encoders

Sector Heads & Data Officers
  • Sector-specific data entry (MSWDO, MAO, MHO, Engineering)
  • Batch data importing via CSV spreadsheets
  • Automatic calculation of gender subtotals and ratios
  • Submit updated data for administrative review
  • Export department data to PDF and CSV tables

Senior Viewers

Executive Leadership (Mayor, SB Members)
  • Read-only access to all municipal dashboards and indicators
  • Real-time visualization of statistical charts and gender splits
  • Interactive GIS map navigation and localized profile viewing
  • One-click PDF and CSV export for sessions and committee hearings
  • Secure viewing without risk of accidental data modification
Core Platform Capabilities

Key Features of the Municipal Portal

State-of-the-art tools enabling the LGU to maintain, expand, and report on data effortlessly.

Sector Dashboards

Visual dashboards for Demographics, Social Development, Economic Development, Infrastructure, Local Governance, and Justice & Peace.

Interactive GIS Map

Official boundary map of Presentacion. Click any barangay to inspect localized population counts, registered farmers, and sector metrics.

Dynamic Table Maker

Allows creation of custom tables anytime (Gender Splits, Subtables, Ratios, Budgets) without programming or external software costs.

Executive Reporting

Instant generation of formatted PDF tables and CSV exports for Sangguniang Bayan council sessions, hearings, and national audits.
