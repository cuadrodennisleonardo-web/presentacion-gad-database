System Objectives & Value

Transforming Municipal Data Management

Overcoming traditional LGU operational bottlenecks through modern municipal cloud architecture.

Traditional Bottlenecks
  • Fragmented paper forms & separate Excel files
  • Inconsistent Sex-Disaggregated Data (SDD)
  • Time-consuming consolidation during AIP drafting
  • Risk of lost baseline data during turnovers
Centralized Platform
  • Single municipal repository accessible 24/7
  • Standardized Male/Female tracking by default
  • Instant 1-click PDF & CSV reporting for SB sessions
  • Structured submission & verification workflow
Executive Impact
  • Faster budget justification with real numbers
  • 100% compliance with DILG, DSWD, DepEd, GAD
  • Clear visibility into sector performance
  • Standardized records for audits and AIP planning

Security & Governance

User Access & Permission Structure

Role-based access control ensuring data integrity, institutional security, and clear accountability.

Super Admin
System Admins / MPDO / IT
  • Full administrative access across all sectors
  • Manage user accounts and sector permissions
  • Create & edit tables via Dynamic Tables Manager
  • Review, approve, or reject department data submissions
  • Inspect municipal audit logs and history
Department Encoders
Sector Heads & Data Officers
  • Sector data entry (MSWDO, MAO, MHO, Engineering)
  • Batch data importing via CSV spreadsheets
  • Automatic calculation of gender subtotals
  • Submit data updates for administrative verification
  • Export department data to PDF and CSV tables
Senior Viewers
Executive Leadership (Mayor, SB)
  • Read-only access to all municipal dashboards
  • Real-time visualization of statistical charts
  • Interactive GIS map navigation and profiles
  • 1-click PDF/CSV export for council sessions
  • Secure viewing without risk of data alteration

Core Platform Capabilities

Key Features of the Municipal Portal

State-of-the-art tools enabling the LGU to maintain, expand, and report on data effortlessly.

Sector Dashboards

Visual dashboards for Demographics, Social Development, Economic Development, Infrastructure, Local Governance, and Justice & Peace.

Interactive GIS Map

Official boundary map of Presentacion. Click any barangay to inspect localized population counts, registered farmers, and sector metrics.

Dynamic Table Maker

Allows creation of custom tables anytime (Gender Splits, Subtables, Ratios, Budgets) without programming or external software costs.

Executive Reporting

Instant generation of formatted PDF tables and CSV exports for Sangguniang Bayan council sessions, hearings, and national audits.
